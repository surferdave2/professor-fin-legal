Kusch Apps LLC ("Kusch Apps," "we," "us") makes Professor Fin. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Professor Fin app and related services. Our Terms of Use also apply.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. - Account details, such as your email address or sign-in details. - Your age check. When you sign in, we ask you to choose the month and year you were born, to confirm you are 13 or older. The answer is checked on your device. We keep only that you answered 13 or older, when, and which version of the screen it was. We do not keep your date of birth, month, or year. If the answer is under 13, we close the account, and your phone keeps a note that this happened so the screen is not simply answered again. - Your choices about AI features. Before anything is sent to an AI provider, we ask for your permission, and we keep a record of which wording you agreed to and when. You can switch AI features off in Settings. - Your agreements. We keep a record of when you agreed to our Terms and Privacy Policy and, each time you share a Photo, of your confirmation about the people in it, with the versions of our texts you saw. - Content you choose to share in Community: Photos, and Tips (written words and catch details such as species, month, and general conditions). - Your fishing activity in the app, such as trips and catches you log, and messages you send to the app's AI guide. - Messages you send us, and reports you make.

Information collected as you use the app. - Device and app information, such as device type, operating system, app version, and diagnostic and usage data. - Location, if you allow it, which we use to give you local forecasts and recommendations. You can turn this off in your device settings. - Purchase status, which we receive from the app store or our billing provider. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Photos. When you share a Photo, we store a cleaned copy with location and camera details removed. We do not keep the original in Community.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide, maintain, and improve Professor Fin; to personalize forecasts and recommendations; to run Community and keep it safe, including the automated photo check described below; to process purchases; to communicate with you about the Service; to answer requests and enforce our Terms; to protect against fraud and abuse; and to comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We do not use Photos for advertising or marketing.

3. Automated photo check

We use an automated check, run by a third-party AI provider, to look at each Photo you share before it is shown. It checks for things that break our rules, including whether people appear. It does not identify anyone, compare faces, or create or store face templates. We may also review Photos ourselves, for example when one is reported. We do not promise that a person will review every Photo.

We use the same kind of automated check on shared text. We ask for your permission before sending anything to an AI provider for the first time, and you can withdraw it in Settings.

4. What other users see

Other users see what you choose to share in Community: Photos and Tips. We never show your account name, email address, exact location, or the exact day or time of a catch. Photo viewers receive short-lived links rather than permanent ones.

5. How we share information

We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers that help us run Professor Fin, such as hosting and storage, AI services (including Anthropic, which receives each shared Photo and shared text so the automated check can run, and the content of messages to the app's AI guide), maps and weather, billing (including RevenueCat), email delivery, and crash and error reporting (Sentry, which receives the error together with the device type, operating system and app version, the time zone, and an anonymous install identifier). They may use information only to provide services to us.
  • Other users, only what you choose to share in Community.
  • Legal and safety reasons, when we believe disclosure is required by law or needed to protect people, Professor Fin, or our rights.
  • Business changes, such as a sale, merger, or financing, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information unless we tell you otherwise.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

The providers we use, and what they receive:

  • Hosting, database, and storage: Google Cloud and Supabase hold your account, your content, and your Photos.
  • AI: Anthropic, as described in Section 3 and for the AI guide.
  • Weather, tide, water, and place data: Open-Meteo (weather), NOAA (tides), USGS (water), OpenStreetMap's Nominatim and Overpass services (place and water lookups), and Zippopotam.us (ZIP code lookups). They receive a location, a place name you typed, or a ZIP code, but not your name, email address, or account.
  • Maps and fonts: MapTiler (map images) and Google Fonts. They receive the map area you view or a font request, and your device's IP address.
  • Sign-in: if you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, that provider learns that you signed in to Professor Fin. We ask Apple for your email address only.
  • Email and billing: Resend (email) and RevenueCat (billing).
  • Crash and error reporting: Sentry, as described above.

6. How long we keep information

We keep information while your account is active and as long as needed for the purposes in this policy.

  • Photos are kept until you unshare them, they are removed, or you delete your account, whichever comes first. We then delete them from our live systems promptly. Copies may remain in routine backups and system logs held by us or our service providers (our own server logs are kept for 30 days) for a limited period in the ordinary course, after which they are overwritten. Our AI provider may keep a copy of a Photo for a limited time under its own policies. We may keep content longer where the law requires or to address safety or abuse issues. A Photo that a reviewer rejects for child safety is not deleted: it is kept hidden from everyone, including after you delete your account, so that it can be reported and preserved where the law requires.
  • Tips can remain after you unshare them or delete your account, without your name, account, location, or exact date. We remove the link to your account, and we will not try to re-identify them or let others do so.
  • Account information is deleted or de-identified when you delete your account, except where we need to keep it for legal, security, or accounting reasons.
  • Conversations with the AI guide are kept so that the guide can continue them, until you delete your account.
  • Moderation records show that a Photo was hidden, reinstated, or removed, and why. They do not contain your name, email address, or account.

7. Your choices and rights

You can unshare anything you shared, delete your account in the app, and turn off location in your device settings. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information by emailing info@kuschapps.com.

Depending on where you live, such as California and other states with privacy laws, you may have additional rights, including to know what we hold about you, to correct or delete it, and to not be treated differently for using these rights. We will respond as the law requires. You may use an authorized agent, and we may need to confirm your identity. If we deny a request, you can ask us to reconsider by replying to our message.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context advertising, as those terms are used in US state privacy laws.

8. Children

Professor Fin is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we will close it and delete the information associated with it. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, please contact us.

Fishing photos often include children, usually posted by a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian can ask us to remove a photo of their child at any time by following the steps on our Copyright and Removal Requests page.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, such as encryption in transit and access controls. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee security.

10. Where information is processed

Professor Fin is operated from the United States, and your information is processed and stored in the United States.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
