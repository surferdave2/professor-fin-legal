Privacy Policy

Professor Fin — Kusch Apps, LLC · Last updated September 2026

Professor Fin ("the app," "we," "us") is a fishing-intelligence app that helps you plan trips, log catches, and get AI-assisted recommendations. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and who it's shared with. We've tried to write it in plain language rather than dense legal boilerplate — if anything here is unclear, contact us using the details at the bottom.

The short version: we collect the minimum needed to run the app — your email, your location (only when you use a feature that needs it), and the fishing content you create. We don't use advertising networks, we don't use behavioral trackers, and we don't sell your data. You can export or permanently delete everything from Settings at any time.

Information We Collect

Account information

To create an account, we collect your email address and a password. We don't collect your name, phone number, or physical address.

Location

Professor Fin uses location to fetch accurate local weather, tide, and season data, and to let you tag your own catches, trips, and saved fishing spots. Location can come from:

  • your device's GPS (read one time when needed, in the foreground only — the app never tracks your location continuously or in the background)
  • a photo's built-in location metadata, if you attach an existing photo to a catch
  • a ZIP code or place name you type in manually

Location tied to a saved catch, trip, or spot is stored as part of your account. A one-off location check that isn't saved to anything (like checking today's forecast) isn't stored.

Content you create

The app stores the fishing content you generate as part of using it, including:

  • catch logs (species, measurements, technique, notes, and optional photos)
  • trips, saved waters, favorite places, and gear presets
  • messages you send to the in-app AI fishing assistant ("Ask Professor Fin")
  • your answers to the onboarding questionnaire (experience level, target species, goals)
  • any feedback you submit through the app

This content is private to your account by default. We don't publish it, make it public, or aggregate it into anything shown to other users.

How We Use Your Information

  • To run the app's core features — authentication, displaying your own data back to you, weather/tide/season lookups, map display.
  • To personalize recommendations — your stated experience level, target species, and your own catch history help tailor AI trip plans and chat responses to you specifically.
  • To improve the app — a small amount of usage data (like how many AI requests you've made that day, used only to enforce fair-use limits) and, separately, an internal count of AI recommendation outcomes we may analyze in the future to improve forecast accuracy.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not use any advertising SDKs, ad networks, or third-party trackers of any kind.

To power specific features, limited pieces of data are sent to outside services that process it on our behalf. None of these services receive your name, email, or account identifier alongside the data described below.

Your private catch notes (the notes field on an individual catch log) are never sent to any third party — they stay in our database and are only ever shown back to you.

Data Storage and Security

Your account data is stored using Supabase (a hosted database and authentication provider) and processed by our backend running on Google Cloud. These are our infrastructure providers, not independent third parties who use your data for their own purposes — think of them as the servers your data lives on, similar to any app's hosting provider.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Export your data — available any time from Settings, includes everything associated with your account.
  • Delete your account — available any time from Settings. This permanently removes your profile, catch logs, trips, photos, chat history, and feedback from our systems.

Children's Privacy

Professor Fin is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll remove it.

In-App Purchases

Professor Fin does not currently offer in-app purchases or subscriptions. If that changes in the future, purchases will be handled through Apple's or Google's own payment systems, and this policy will be updated to reflect any additional data that involves.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to how we collect or use your information, we'll update this page and revise the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to info@kuschapps.com, or through the support page.
