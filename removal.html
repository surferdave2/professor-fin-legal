Copyright and Removal Requests

Professor Fin
Last updated: October 7, 2026

This page explains how to ask us to remove content from Professor Fin. It is part of our Terms of Use.

1. A photo that shows you or your child

Anyone shown in a Photo, including a person who does not use Professor Fin, and the parent or guardian of a child shown, can ask us to remove it.

How to ask. Use the Report button on the Shared Catch in the app, or email info@kuschapps.com. Please tell us which Photo you mean (a screenshot helps) and how to reach you. You do not need to explain why.

What happens. We respond in a timely way. We may remove the Photo first and review the request afterward.

Intimate images shared without consent. If a Photo is an intimate image of you shared without your consent, we remove it no later than 48 hours after we receive a valid request. A valid request is in writing, with your physical or electronic signature, and includes:

  • enough information for us to find the content, such as a screenshot or the name of the Shared Catch;
  • a brief statement that you believe in good faith that the image was shared without your consent; and
  • your contact information.

If your request is missing something, tell us anyway and we will work with you.

2. Copyright

We respect copyright. If you believe content on Professor Fin infringes your copyright, send a notice to our designated agent:

Copyright Agent
Kusch Apps LLC
5534 Saint Joe Road, Fort Wayne, IN 46835
info@kuschapps.com

Your notice must include:

  1. your physical or electronic signature, or that of a person authorized to act for you;
  2. a description of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed;
  3. a description of the content you claim is infringing and where it appears, with enough detail for us to find it;
  4. your name, address, telephone number, and email address;
  5. a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law; and
  6. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act for the owner.

Please be aware that a person who knowingly misrepresents that content is infringing may be liable for damages under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f).

When we receive a complete notice, we act promptly to remove or disable access to the content and tell the person who shared it.

Counter-notice

If you shared content that was removed and you believe the removal was a mistake or the content was misidentified, you may send a counter-notice to the Copyright Agent above. It must include:

  1. your physical or electronic signature;
  2. a description of the content that was removed and where it appeared before removal;
  3. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the content was removed by mistake or misidentification;
  4. your name, address, and telephone number; and
  5. a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court for the district where you live (or, if outside the United States, any district where we can be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice.

When we receive a complete counter-notice, we send it to the person who sent the original notice. Unless that person tells us within 10 business days that they have filed a court action, we will restore the content in 10 to 14 business days.

Repeat infringers

We have a policy of ending the accounts of users who are repeat infringers, in appropriate circumstances.

3. Other reports

You can report any other content that breaks our rules using the Report button in the app, or by email at info@kuschapps.com.
